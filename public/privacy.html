Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This page explains what information Hourly Forecast collects when you use the site, and how it is used.

Location and searches

When you search for a city, the name you type is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find matching places. When you choose "Use my location", your browser asks for permission first; if you allow it, your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast. We don't store your location on our servers.

Your last chosen place and your preferred units are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them next time. You can clear them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense and by advertisers we work with directly.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes basic request data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.
